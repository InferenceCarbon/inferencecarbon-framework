## Provenance rule

Every value in these workbooks is either campaign-measured (BLUE on the Corpus sheet; regenerated from the
frozen corpus by `reproduce/corpus_summary.py`), a typed parameter with a source row in `../../parameters/`
(BLUE on Assumptions, GridIntensity and ShapeCorrection), or a formula. Known defects in the Corpus sheet
of v1.0.0 are listed in `../../VERIFICATION.md` §4.
